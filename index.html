FEATURED PROJECT · STARTED LAST MONTH Active Development (Oct 2026 – Present)

Lexiva — Living AI Dictionary & Adaptive Learning Ecosystem

Turns traditional dictionaries into a living, personalized learning system powered by FSRS spaced repetition, AI leech coaching, and multi-accent neural audio.

Architected as a high-throughput backend (Kotlin 2.4, Ktor 3.5, Exposed, PostgreSQL) paired with a modern Android client. Lexiva understands learner queries, aggregates deep multi-dictionary sources with web & AI fallback, and tailors every explanation, story, and quiz to the user's CEFR level, culture, and interests.

Kotlin 2.4 Ktor 3.5 Jetpack Compose FSRS v4 PostgreSQL & Exposed Liara AI / DeepSeek SearXNG & Crawl4AI Docker & Caddy
🔍

Deep Multi-Source Search & RAG

Aggregates curated lexicons, SearXNG web search, and Crawl4AI markdown extraction with LLM synthesis to deliver rich, contextual word insights.

🧠

Adaptive FSRS Spaced Repetition

Cutting-edge Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler powering flashcard boxes across 5 interactive formats: MCQ, Flip, Speak, Dictation, and Unscramble.

💡

AI Leech Coach & Diagnostics

Nightly analysis detects persistent memory lapses, generating personalized mnemonics, visual metaphors, and contrastive memory anchors.

🎙️

Word Stories & Scene Dialogues

AI-generated graded micro-stories (A1–C2) and two-person spoken dialogues with multi-accent neural TTS in US, UK, and Australian accents.

📊

Indicative IELTS Assessment

Automated evaluation for IELTS Writing Task 1/2 and Speaking with speech-to-text, words-per-minute metrics, and CEFR criterion bands.

📱

Cryptographic Offline-First Sync

Ed25519-signed offline licenses and delta manifests enabling seamless on-device study and review replay with zero network connection.
